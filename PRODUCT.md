# Sarf (صرف)

register: product

## Product Purpose
Sarf answers the question millions of Algerians ask every single day: "combien est l'euro au square aujourd'hui ?" Algeria runs on a dual exchange market: the official Bank of Algeria rate (EUR ≈ 152 DZD) and the parallel "square" rate (EUR ≈ 277 DZD, an 82% premium). Diaspora sending money home, students paying for visas, importers, travelers, families: everyone checks the square rate before moving a single euro. Sarf shows both rates side by side, converts amounts honestly (bank vs square difference in dinars), tracks history, and alerts on thresholds.

## Users
- Diaspora (France, Canada, UK, Gulf) deciding when and how to send money home. Checks at night on the phone.
- Locals in Algeria holding or needing hard currency: travelers (allocation touristique), students (Campus France), importers.
- Habit profile: 15-second glance, 1–3× daily. Numbers first, everything else second.

## Language & Locale
French primary, Arabic (RTL) full parity. DZD amounts formatted with thin spaces (27 700 DA). Dates in French/Arabic. Buy = achat (cambiste buys your devise), sell = vente (cambiste sells devise to you): label from the user's point of view to avoid the classic confusion.

## Tone
A precise, unexcited exchange-board. States numbers, dates and sources; never hypes, never advises ("bon moment pour vendre" is banned). Transparency: every figure carries its as-of date and source; missing data shows as missing, never invented.

## Anti-references
- Crypto-app neon-on-black with green/red flashing tickers.
- Finance navy + gold "wealth management" gravitas.
- The existing DZ rate sites: ad-farm blogspam, popups, ten AdSense blocks per screen.

## Strategic principles
- Accuracy is the brand: aggregated from published sources (ExchangeDZ, ObservAlgérie/TSA, Bank of Algeria), each figure dated. Rates vary by city and negotiation; Sarf says so.
- Data pipeline is the moat-in-progress: automated daily capture builds the historical dataset nobody else exposes cleanly.
- Zero-friction glance: the hero number is readable before the page finishes settling. No interstitials ever.
- Legal posture: information service like the national press; no transactions, no exchange offers (explicit disclaimer).
